**RQ1.** Which aspects of their careers do these workers feel unable or still able to control?

**RQ2.** How do these workers describe the direction, intensity and persistence of their career effort?

**RQ3.** How do these workers link their career responses to their resources, others' evaluations and the idea of "lying flat"?

**Aim.** This study aims to explore how young early-career workers in Ho Chi Minh City relate experiences of social powerlessness to their career motivation.

**Objectives**

To identify which aspects of their careers these workers feel unable or still able to control.

1.  To explore how these workers describe the direction, intensity and persistence of their career effort.

2.  To explore how these workers link their career responses to their resources, others' evaluations and the idea of "lying flat".
